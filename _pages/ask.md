Ask anything about how **STAT-2311 Mathematical Statistics I** runs this term — dates,
assessment, deadlines, policies — or about a topic from the lectures. The assistant
searches the [course page](/teaching/2026-fall-mathematical-statistics-I) and this
term's lecture material and answers by quoting them.

It runs entirely inside your browser. Your questions are not sent anywhere, not stored,
and not seen by anyone, including me.

{% include course-chat.html %}

### What it can and cannot do

It is good at the things the syllabus states plainly: when the quizzes and midterms are,
what each is worth, when problem sets close, where my office is, which sections a given
week covers, and which lecture introduced a particular idea.

It cannot see your grades, grant extensions, predict what will be on a paper, or work
problems out for you. For any of those, come to office hours on Wednesday between 16:00
and 18:00, or e-mail me at [sorujov@ada.edu.az](mailto:sorujov@ada.edu.az).

Where it disagrees with the syllabus, the syllabus is right — tell me and I will fix it.
