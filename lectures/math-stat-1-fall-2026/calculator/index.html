Two or three events

Fill the 2×2 table from any three facts, test independence, and get the bounds when only P(A) and P(B) are known.

Bounds: max(0, P(A)+P(B)−1) ≤ P(A∩B) ≤ min(P(A), P(B)) and max(P(A), P(B)) ≤ P(A∪B) ≤ min(1, P(A)+P(B)).

Inclusion–exclusion: P(A∪B∪C) = P(A)+P(B)+P(C) − P(A∩B) − P(A∩C) − P(B∩C) + P(A∩B∩C).
